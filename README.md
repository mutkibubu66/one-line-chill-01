## The line

> My phone buzzed with a photo of me asleep, taken from the foot of my bed, timestamped three minutes from now.

## Why it works

The scare is built from ordinary things — a phone buzz, a photo, your own bed — so the reader pictures it instantly. The twist is withheld until the last words: the photo was taken from inside the room, and its timestamp is three minutes in the future, which means the thing standing at the foot of the bed has not finished yet. The dread outlasts the line because the reader is left waiting for those three minutes to run out.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Pure standard library Python 3, no network. It validates the line (exactly one line, 1–200 characters, valid UTF-8, no leading or trailing whitespace) and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.
